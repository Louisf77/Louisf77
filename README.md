### 👋 About me

I'm a full stack engineer working across **React/TypeScript** and **Ruby on Rails** on a multi-region fintech platform at [Hnry](https://hnry.co.uk) (UK, AU, NZ). I joined as one of the first two engineers on the UK team, which is now eight strong.

- 🏦 I own delivery end to end, from hard integrations (a UK banking and card provider, identity verification across three countries, HMRC) through to the product built on top of them
- 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 I line-manage three engineers and run engineering chapter sessions
- 🤖 I lead AI engineering for the UK business: Claude Code plugins, skills, hooks, MCP integrations and agent workflows
- 🛠️ Before software, I studied Product Design & Manufacture Engineering at Nottingham, which still shapes how I think about building things people actually use
